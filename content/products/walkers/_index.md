Regaining the ability to stand and walk is one of the most meaningful milestones in patient recovery. Bure walkers — designed by DHG in Sweden — are built to support that process safely, with construction and ergonomics informed by decades of rehabilitation practice.

The Bure range supports patients with reduced lower body strength through the full progression from supported standing to independent ambulation. Three models cover adult and bariatric weight capacities, with designs suited to rehabilitation programs, long-term care, and home environments.

Available in North and South America through TR Group, Inc. authorized dealer partners.

**Common questions:**
- **What is the difference between the Rise & Go DB and the Standtall?** — The Rise & Go DB is designed to assist patients in rising from seated to standing and walking. The Standtall focuses on supported standing and balance training.
- **Is there a bariatric walker option?** — Yes. The Bure XL is rated to 529 lbs (240 kg) and designed for bariatric rehabilitation.
- **Are these available for home use?** — Contact an authorized dealer — some models are suitable for residential environments with appropriate clinical assessment.
