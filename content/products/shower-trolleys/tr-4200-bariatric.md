## Overview

The TR-4200 Atlas Junior bridges the gap between the standard-capacity TR-3200 and the full bariatric TR-4000 Atlas. With a 595 lbs (270 kg) capacity and a narrower 29 in mattress width, it is the right choice when patient weight exceeds the 507 lbs (230 kg) TR-3200 rating but space constraints rule out the wider TR-4000 Atlas (36 in).

Electric height and tilt are controlled via hand pendant.

## Specifying Bariatric Equipment

When specifying between the TR-4200 Atlas Junior and TR-4000 Atlas, the primary considerations are:

1. **Patient weight** — TR-4200 covers up to 595 lbs (270 kg); TR-4000 covers up to 992 lbs (450 kg)
2. **Room width** — TR-4200 at 29 in may navigate corridors and doorways that the 36 in TR-4000 cannot
3. **Mattress surface** — TR-4000's wider surface provides more support for very large patients

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is the TR-4200 appropriate for general use, or only bariatric programs?**
The TR-4200 is a reinforced-frame bariatric trolley — it is rated and built for heavier loads than standard-capacity models. It is appropriate in any environment where patient weight may approach or exceed 507 lbs (230 kg), including general acute wards that admit a range of patient sizes.

**What makes the TR-4200's frame different from the standard TR-3200?**
The TR-4200 uses a reinforced bariatric frame designed to handle the dynamic loads of heavier patients during transfers and bathing. The castors are also heavy-duty rated. This structural difference is why the weight capacity steps up from 507 lbs (230 kg) to 595 lbs (270 kg), and why the TR-4200 is specified over the TR-3200 for heavier patient populations.
