  <p class="lp-section-label">Why It Matters</p>
  <h2>Built So Care Never Feels Like a Procedure</h2>
  <p class="lp-section-intro">Every unnecessary transfer is a moment where the patient becomes cargo, not a person. Shower and commode chairs that combine transport, bathing, and toileting in one keep patients upright and present, so the caregiver can stay focused on the person, not the logistics.</p>
  <div class="lp-benefit-grid">
    <div class="lp-benefit-card">
      <strong>One seat for the whole routine</strong>
      <p>Transport, shower, and toilet without asking the patient to transfer - fewer lifts, less risk, and a hygiene routine that flows rather than interrupts.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="lp-benefit-card">
      <strong>Dignity built into the design</strong>
      <p>Seated and upright, patients participate in their own care - not something done to them, but something done with them.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="lp-benefit-card">
      <strong>Open access for the caregiver</strong>
      <p>Height-adjustable frames and open-access seating put the caregiver in a safe, ergonomic working position at every step of the routine.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="lp-benefit-card">
      <strong>A chair for every patient</strong>
      <p>Standard, positioning, and bariatric models support a broad range of patient sizes, postures, and care environments.</p>

  <div class="lp-faq-item">
    <p class="lp-faq-q">Can I get a price or place an order online?</p>
    <p class="lp-faq-a">TR Group routes pricing, ordering, and availability requests through authorized distributor partners. Review the models above, then use the form below and we will help get your request to the right purchasing path.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="lp-faq-item">
    <p class="lp-faq-q">What is the difference between a shower chair and a commode chair?</p>
    <p class="lp-faq-a">A shower chair is designed for seated bathing. A commode chair combines toileting and showering in one seat so the user does not need to transfer between the two. That single change reduces handling risk and keeps the user more comfortable throughout the routine.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="lp-faq-item">
    <p class="lp-faq-q">Can TR Group help me find the right model for my patient or facility?</p>
    <p class="lp-faq-a">Yes. Share your care setting, user weight, support needs, and any specific requirements in the inquiry form below. TR Group will route your inquiry to a distributor who can advise on model fit and availability.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="lp-faq-item">
    <p class="lp-faq-q">Do you carry bariatric models, and are any suitable for home care?</p>
    <p class="lp-faq-a">Yes. The Rebotec Dallas and Phoenix 200 support users up to 441 lbs (200 kg), and several TR Equipment and Rebotec models may fit home-care use. Share the user's weight, bathroom constraints, and care setting so a distributor can guide the right fit.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="lp-faq-item">
    <p class="lp-faq-q">Do these chairs support infection control and safe patient handling goals?</p>
    <p class="lp-faq-a">Yes. Rebotec frames use solid construction rather than hollow tubing, reducing hidden areas where moisture and contaminants can collect. Chairs that reduce unnecessary transfers also support safer patient handling workflows by lowering the number of manual handling events during hygiene care.</p>
