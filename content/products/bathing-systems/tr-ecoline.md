## Overview

The TR Ecoline delivers core clinical bathing functionality — thermostatic water control and clinical-grade construction — in a fixed-height configuration. Without a height adjustment mechanism, it is the most cost-efficient entry point in TR Equipment's bathing system range, suited to facilities where caregiver height variation is managed through other means or where capital cost is the primary constraint.

It's the right choice for facilities that:

- Need reliable, clinical-grade bathing capability
- Have standard (non-bariatric, non-complex) patient populations
- Are optimizing across a multiple-unit procurement

## Who Is This For?

- Acute hospital units where bathing is a clinical hygiene task rather than a therapeutic or comfort-focused activity
- Multi-bed skilled nursing facilities specifying multiple units across bathing rooms
- Facilities with clearly defined standard patient populations (no bariatric, no complex transfer needs)

## When to Specify Ecoline vs. Comfortline

Choose the **Ecoline** when the primary goal is efficient patient hygiene in a standard care setting. Choose the **Comfortline** when patient comfort, therapeutic applications, or person-centered care are priorities — typically in long-term care, palliative, or memory care environments.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does the Ecoline include thermostatic water temperature control?**
Yes. Thermostatic temperature control is standard across the TR Equipment bathing system range including the Ecoline — it is not an optional extra. Safe temperature limiting is a baseline clinical requirement, not a premium feature.

**Can the Ecoline be upgraded with therapeutic features later?**
No. The Ecoline is a fixed specification. If whirlpool, air spa, or heated surfaces are required now or are likely to be required in the future, the [Comfortline](/products/bathing-systems/tr-comfortline/) is the appropriate base specification.
