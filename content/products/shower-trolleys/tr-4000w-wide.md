## Overview

The TR-4000-W Atlas Wide is the maximum-specification bariatric shower trolley in the TR Equipment range. It shares the 992 lbs (450 kg) capacity and electric height-and-tilt functionality of the TR-4000 Atlas, but extends the mattress width to 42 in — providing exceptional support surface for the largest bariatric patients.

The extra width is the key differentiator from the standard TR-4000 Atlas (36 in). For patients who require the full 42 in surface, the TR-4000-W is the clinical specification of choice.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**When does the extra width of the TR-4000-W matter clinically?**
The 42 in mattress surface of the TR-4000-W provides meaningful additional lateral support for patients whose body width exceeds what the 36 in TR-4000 Atlas can comfortably accommodate. For very large patients, a wider surface reduces the risk of limbs extending over the edge during bathing and provides better distribution of patient weight across the trolley surface.

**Does the TR-4000-W fit through standard bariatric room doorways?**
At 42 in wide, the TR-4000-W requires bariatric-configured doorways (typically 60 in or wider in purpose-built bariatric suites). Standard clinical doorways that accommodate the 36 in TR-4000 may not accommodate the TR-4000-W. Facility planning should confirm clearances before specification.

**Is the capacity the same as the standard TR-4000 Atlas?**
Yes — both the TR-4000 Atlas (36 in) and TR-4000-W Atlas Wide (42 in) are rated to 992 lbs (450 kg). The wider model is specified when patient size requires additional surface width, not additional weight capacity.
