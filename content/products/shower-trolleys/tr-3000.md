## Overview

The TR-3000 Mod 21 is TR Equipment's most widely specified shower trolley, combining battery-powered electric height adjustment with manual tilt in a robust, clinically proven package. The hand pendant control lets caregivers adjust height with one hand — keeping the other hand free for patient support.

Electric height adjustment reduces physical caregiver effort and enables precise working height — important for facilities with high patient throughput or staff with musculoskeletal concerns.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does the battery last between charges?**
Battery life depends on usage volume and the frequency of height adjustments per session. In typical clinical use, a fully charged battery supports multiple patient bathing sessions per charge. TR Equipment recommends returning the unit to its charging station when not in use.

**Is the TR-3000 suitable for a bariatric patient population?**
The TR-3000 is rated to 440 lbs (200 kg), which covers the majority of clinical patient weights. For patients above 440 lbs (200 kg), the TR-4200 Atlas Junior (595 lbs / 270 kg) or TR-4000 Atlas (992 lbs / 450 kg) are the appropriate specifications.

**Does the electric system require fixed wiring or an outlet connection during use?**
No. The TR-3000 is battery-operated — no mains connection is required during patient use. The battery charges via a standard outlet when the unit is docked between uses.

**What is IPX5 protection, and why does it matter?**
IPX5 means the electrical components are protected against water jets from any direction. In a clinical shower room where the trolley is in use under active water flow, this rating ensures the electric height mechanism is not damaged by routine water exposure.
