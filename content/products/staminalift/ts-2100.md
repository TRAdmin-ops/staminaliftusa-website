## Overview

The StaminaLift TS-2100 is the original hospital bed mover from RIHA Industries — the product that established StaminaLift as the market leader in powered bed transport. Like all StaminaLift models, it is built around two core capabilities: **push/pull drive** and **lift-and-attach**.

**Push/pull** is the primary function — the electric drive propels the fully loaded bed through corridors, around corners, and up ramps under complete joystick control, with no physical pushing or steering from the operator. **Lift** refers to the mechanism that raises the attachment point during connection, allowing the unit to take up the load.

The key distinction between the TS-2100 and the TS-5000 is the attachment method. The TS-2100 connects to the **bed frame**, while the TS-5000 uses a fully patented jaw mechanism that grips the **caster wheel** directly. Because caster wheel sizes and configurations are broadly standardized, the TS-5000 achieves compatibility with 95% of hospital beds globally. The TS-2100's frame-based attachment works reliably where it fits, but is less flexible across different bed models and manufacturers.

## Dual Capability: Push/Pull and Lift

The TS-2100 operates on the same principle as the TS-5000 and TS-6000:

- **Push/pull capacity** governs how much total load (bed + patient + equipment) the unit can safely transport across flat surfaces and inclines. This is the number that determines whether a single operator can move a fully loaded bed without physical strain.
- **Lift capacity** governs the attachment mechanism — how much weight the unit can raise during connection. This is what allows the mover to engage the bed and take up the load.
