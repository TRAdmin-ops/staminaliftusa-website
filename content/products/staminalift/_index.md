On any given workday, healthcare staff move hospital beds an average of 9 mi (15 km) — up to 46 mi (75 km) per week. A fully loaded bed can weigh 1,100 lbs (500 kg). Repeated manual pushing and steering of that load is a leading cause of musculoskeletal injury in clinical settings.

Powered patient transport eliminates that risk. The StaminaLift range by RIHA Industries — the market-leading hospital bed movers — connects to the bed via a patented caster-jaw mechanism and moves it under full joystick control, with zero physical pushing required. A single operator. No strain.

The TS-5000 and TS-6000 are FDA-registered — the only hospital bed movers with that designation in the United States. Available in North America through TR Group, Inc. authorized dealer partners.
