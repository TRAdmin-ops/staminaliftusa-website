Non-ambulatory patients require safe, dignified hygiene care — and the equipment caregivers use directly determines how physically demanding and injury-prone that care is. [TR Equipment](https://www.trequipment.com/) shower trolleys are engineered in Sweden around one principle: make it easier to deliver good care without hurting the person delivering it.

Every model provides height adjustment to match caregiver working height — eliminating the back-loading of fixed-height alternatives — and a tilt function for hair washing and full drainage. All models are manufactured in Sweden and available in North America exclusively through TR Group, Inc.

**Six models** cover the full range of clinical requirements, from standard adult care to dedicated bariatric environments at 992 lbs (450 kg) capacity. Use the comparison table at the bottom of this page to identify the right model for your facility.

**Common questions:**
- **Which model do I need for bariatric patients?** — The TR-4200 Atlas Junior covers up to 595 lbs (270 kg), the TR-4000 Atlas to 992 lbs (450 kg), and the TR-4000-W Atlas Wide to 992 lbs (450 kg) with a 42 in mattress surface.
- **What is the difference between hydraulic and electric height adjustment?** — Hydraulic (TR-2000) is foot-operated with no battery required. Electric (TR-3000 and above) uses a hand pendant and battery — faster adjustment and less physical effort per transfer.
- **Do I need electric tilt?** — Electric tilt (TR-3200 and above) gives caregivers full one-handed positioning control. Manual tilt (TR-2000, TR-3000) is adjusted by hand before locking.
