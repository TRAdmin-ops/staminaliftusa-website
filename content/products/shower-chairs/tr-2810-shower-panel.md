## Overview

The TR-2810 Shower Panel is a purpose-designed water delivery system for clinical shower rooms equipped with TR Equipment shower trolleys. Unlike standard domestic shower fittings, the TR-2810 is built for the ergonomic and hygiene demands of daily clinical patient bathing.

The thermostatic mixer valve limits water temperature to prevent scalding — a critical safety feature when bathing patients with reduced skin sensation (including those with neurological conditions, diabetes, or advanced age-related changes).

## Integration with TR Equipment Trolleys

The TR-2810 is designed to work alongside the TR-2000, TR-3000, TR-3200, and bariatric trolley series. The adjustable shower arm accommodates the varying working heights of electric trolleys, allowing caregivers to position the water delivery point appropriately for each patient and task.

## Specifying Shower Room Equipment

When planning a new or refurbished shower room for TR Equipment trolleys, a complete specification typically includes:

- TR shower trolley (model based on patient weight and required adjustment)
- TR-2810 shower panel (or equivalent thermostatic water delivery)
- TR-1000 shower chair (for ambulatory patients using the same room)
- Appropriate drainage, non-slip flooring, and transfer space
