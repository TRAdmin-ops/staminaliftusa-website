## Overview

The TR-1000 is TR Equipment's standard shower chair — a robust, adjustable seat designed for assisted bathing in clinical and home care settings. The anodized aluminum frame provides excellent corrosion resistance in wet environments, while the antibacterial seat material simplifies infection control cleaning routines.

Swing-away armrests facilitate lateral transfers from wheelchairs and hoists, and the adjustable seat height accommodates users of different heights and allows optimal positioning during bathing.

## Who Is This For?

- Skilled nursing facilities and assisted living with ambulatory or partially ambulatory residents
- Home care patients who require seating assistance during showering
- Accessible shower rooms requiring a durable, cleanable seat option

## Maintenance

The TR-1000 is designed for minimal maintenance. The aluminum frame should be rinsed and wiped down after each use. Seat surfaces are compatible with standard hospital-grade disinfectants — consult the product IFU for approved cleaning agents.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**When is a shower chair used instead of a shower trolley?**
A shower chair is appropriate for patients who can sit upright independently or with minimal support, and who can be safely seated during washing. A shower trolley is required when patients cannot be seated — either due to physical dependency, contractures, spinal conditions, or clinical protocols requiring the supine position during bathing. A facility serving a mixed population typically needs both.

**Does the TR-1000 fit inside a TR Equipment shower trolley room?**
Yes. The TR-1000 is designed to be used in the same clinical shower room as TR Equipment trolleys. The [TR-2810 shower panel](/products/shower-chairs/tr-2810-shower-panel/) is designed to deliver water to both the trolley and the chair depending on which is in use.

**Are the armrests removable or only swing-away?**
The TR-1000 features swing-away armrests — they pivot out of the way to allow lateral transfer from a wheelchair or hoist sling without removal. Fully removable armrests are an option on some configurations; confirm with your dealer.

**Is the TR-1000 suitable for home care as well as clinical settings?**
Yes. The TR-1000's corrosion-resistant aluminum frame, adjustable height, and foldable design make it appropriate for home care bathrooms as well as clinical facilities. The open seat design supports perineal hygiene care in both settings.
