## Overview

The TR-900 is TR Equipment's full-size height-adjustable bathtub — designed for nursing homes and care facilities where assisted bathing comfort and caregiver ergonomics are priorities. Height adjustment via hand control raises or lowers the tub to match each caregiver, reducing back strain during patient bathing routines.

The glass fibre construction is warm to the touch, lightweight, and appropriate for most care environments.

## Who Is This For?

- Nursing homes and long-term care facilities where immersion bathing is part of the care model
- Assisted living with a defined resident bathing program
- Care facilities with adequate bathroom dimensions for the 92 × 33 in footprint
- Facilities specifying a durable, standard-capacity bathing solution without electric height adjustment

## Comparison with TR-900 Stainless Steel

The standard TR-900 uses a glass fibre tub. If your facility requires enhanced chemical resistance or a material that withstands high-frequency disinfection protocols, consider the [TR-900 Stainless Steel](/products/bathing-systems/tr-900-stainless/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are the space requirements for the TR-900?**
The TR-900 has a 92 × 33 in footprint. Additionally, clear working space on at least one long side is required for caregiver access during bathing. Height adjustment raises the tub to 37 in — ceiling height is not typically a constraint, but overhead clearance should be confirmed for facilities with suspended systems.

**Is the TR-900 suitable for patients who cannot stand?**
Yes. The height-adjustable frame allows the tub to be lowered for patient transfer — typically used alongside a patient hoist or ceiling lift. TR Equipment accessories including hoisting slings and transfer systems are compatible with the TR-900. Contact us to discuss full bathing room configuration.

**Why glass fibre rather than acrylic or stainless steel?**
Glass fibre is warm to the touch (unlike stainless steel), lightweight, and durable in standard care environments. It is the right material where surface warmth matters for patient comfort and where high-concentration disinfectants are not in routine use. For infection-sensitive environments, see the [TR-900 Stainless Steel](/products/bathing-systems/tr-900-stainless/).

**Does the TR-900 require a fixed water connection?**
Yes. The TR-900 is a fixed-installation bathing system — it requires plumbing for hot and cold water supply and a drain connection. Unlike shower trolleys, it is not a portable unit. Installation specifications are detailed in the product IFU.
