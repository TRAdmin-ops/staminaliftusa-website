## Overview

The TR Spaceline addresses one of the most common challenges in healthcare renovation: fitting a clinically capable bathing system into a room that wasn't originally designed for it. The Spaceline's compact footprint delivers full patient bathing capability without requiring a full-size bath room footprint.

This makes the Spaceline the default choice for:

- Retrofit installations in existing nursing home or hospital rooms
- Older facilities where bathrooms were designed for domestic rather than clinical use
- New builds with constrained bathroom allocations

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What does "compact footprint" mean in practical dimensions?**
The Spaceline is designed for rooms where the TR-900 (92 in) or TR-1700 (66 in) cannot be accommodated. Exact Spaceline dimensions vary by configuration — contact us for full installation specifications and room planning support.

**Is the Spaceline appropriate for new-build installations or only retrofit?**
Both. While the Spaceline's compact design is frequently chosen for retrofit into existing bathrooms, it is equally appropriate for new builds where bathroom dimensions are constrained by building layout. For new construction, sharing the Spaceline's installation specifications with your project architect early avoids downstream spec changes.

**Does the Spaceline support hoist and transfer accessories?**
Yes. The Spaceline is compatible with TR Equipment hoisting and transfer accessories. For complex transfer needs or fully dependent patients, contact us to discuss a complete bathing room configuration including ceiling hoist compatibility.
