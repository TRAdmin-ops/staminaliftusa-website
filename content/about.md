## Swedish Engineering for North American Healthcare

TR Group, Inc. brings Scandinavia's leading clinical patient hygiene and rehabilitation equipment to hospitals, skilled nursing facilities, and rehabilitation centers across the United States and Canada.

Our core brands — TR Equipment and Bure — are designed and manufactured in Sweden, where a tradition of purposeful, human-centered engineering has shaped healthcare equipment design for decades. Swedish design philosophy holds that every element must serve a function: no decorative weight, no unnecessary complexity, only what clinical care actually requires. That principle is visible in every height range, every tilt mechanism, every weight rating across our product catalog.

## Why Scandinavian Design Matters in Healthcare

Equipment designed around the caregiver protects the caregiver. Adjustable working heights eliminate the back-loading of fixed-height alternatives. Ergonomic handle positions reduce cumulative fatigue. Every TR Equipment product is hand-assembled in Sweden — a level of craftsmanship that shows up in service life. Facilities regularly report TR Equipment trolleys and bathing systems in active daily clinical use long after comparable mass-produced alternatives have needed replacement.

Scandinavian healthcare equipment manufacturers have been solving these problems in European clinical environments for decades. TR Group, Inc. makes that engineering accessible to North American facilities through an authorized dealer network across the US and Canada.

## Our Brands

**[TR Equipment](https://www.trequipment.com/) (Sweden)** — The core of our catalog. TR Equipment has been developing shower trolleys, bathing systems, and patient lifts for clinical use in Sweden for decades. Every unit is hand-assembled — not mass-produced — and engineered for demanding daily use in hospitals and long-term care facilities.

**DHG / Bure Walkers (Sweden)** — DHG is the parent company behind the Bure brand. Bure walkers support rehabilitation and independent mobility — the Rise & Go DB, Bure XL, and Standtall are available in the Americas through TR Group, Inc.

**StaminaLift by RIHA Industries (Australia)** — FDA-registered hospital bed movers that eliminate the musculoskeletal injuries caused by manual bed transport. TS-5000 and TS-6000 models available through TR Group, Inc.

**[Rebotec](https://www.rebotec.de/en/) (Germany)** — German-engineered shower and commode chairs, pediatric walkers, and crutches. The full Rebotec North America catalog is available at [rebotecus.com](https://rebotecus.com/).
