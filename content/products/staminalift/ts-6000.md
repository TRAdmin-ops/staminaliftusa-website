## Overview

The StaminaLift TS-6000 is the bariatric-rated hospital bed mover in the StaminaLift range. With a **1,984 lbs (900 kg) push/pull capacity**, it can transport nearly a ton of loaded bariatric bed weight up a 7° incline under full load — a capability unmatched in the healthcare industry.

The TS-6000 is designed specifically for bariatric and acute care environments where bed weight — including patient, equipment, and the bed frame itself — routinely exceeds what the TS-5000 is rated to move. The design eliminates the need for bending or strapping during attachment, directly addressing the manual handling risks that cause injury to staff in bariatric care settings.

All features of the TS-5000 are included: joystick control, Bluetooth, 360° turning radius, three customizable drive programs, automatic braking, and key-only security.

## Comparing TS-5000 and TS-6000

| | TS-5000 | TS-6000 |
|---|---|---|
| **Push/pull capacity** | **1,323 lbs (600 kg)** | **1,984 lbs (900 kg)** |
| Lift capacity | 661 lbs (300 kg) | 992 lbs (450 kg) |
| Max gradient | 7° | 7° |
| Primary use | General hospital beds | Bariatric and acute care beds |

**Push/pull capacity** is the force applied to move the loaded bed across the floor — the rating that governs safe single-operator transport. **Lift capacity** is the vertical load the caster-jaw mechanism handles when lifting the caster during attachment. Both ratings are maintained at the full 7° operating gradient.

## When to Specify the TS-6000

- Bariatric units where bed + patient weight regularly exceeds 1,323 lbs (600 kg) total
- Acute care and ICU environments with heavily equipped beds (monitors, pumps, ventilators)
- Facilities with long transport distances on grades or ramps
- Any setting where the TS-5000's push/pull rating is insufficient for the bed configuration
