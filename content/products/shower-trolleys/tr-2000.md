## Overview

The TR-2000 Mod 21 is TR Equipment's entry-level clinical shower trolley — a practical, durable choice for facilities that need reliable patient bathing equipment without the complexity of electric height adjustment.

Foot-operated hydraulic height adjustment raises and lowers the trolley surface to match caregiver height, freeing both hands for patient care during repositioning. The tilt function allows head-down positioning for hair washing and full drainage.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the difference between hydraulic and electric height adjustment?**
Hydraulic adjustment requires the caregiver to operate a foot pump to raise the trolley and release a valve to lower it. Electric adjustment (on the TR-3000) uses a hand pendant — one button raises, one lowers. Hydraulic is reliable and requires no battery charging; electric reduces physical effort and is faster in high-throughput settings.

**Does the TR-2000 require an electrical outlet?**
No. The TR-2000 is fully non-electric — foot-operated hydraulic mechanism, manual tilt. It operates anywhere without power infrastructure.

**What drain or plumbing requirements does the TR-2000 have?**
The TR-2000 requires standard clinical shower drainage below the trolley. No fixed plumbing connects to the trolley itself — water delivery is handled separately by a shower arm or panel (see the [TR-2810 Shower Panel](/products/shower-chairs/tr-2810-shower-panel/)).

**Can the TR-2000 surfaces be disinfected?**
Yes. The powder-coated frame and stainless steel fittings are compatible with standard hospital-grade disinfectants. The slatted mattress support and any mattress/pad accessories should be cleaned per the IFU and the accessory manufacturer's cleaning protocol.
