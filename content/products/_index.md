TR Group, Inc. is the exclusive North American distributor for [TR Equipment](https://www.trequipment.com/) and Bure — Swedish manufacturers of clinical patient hygiene and rehabilitation equipment — and the US distributor for [Rebotec](https://www.rebotec.de/en/) (Germany) and StaminaLift (Australia).

Every product in this catalog was designed to solve a specific clinical problem: reducing caregiver injury, supporting patient dignity, enabling safe transfer, or assisting in rehabilitation. Swedish engineering tradition runs through all of it — purposeful design, hand-assembled construction, and equipment built for long service life in demanding daily clinical environments.
