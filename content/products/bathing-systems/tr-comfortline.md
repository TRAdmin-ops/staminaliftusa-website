## Overview

The TR Comfortline prioritizes patient comfort alongside clinical function. The ergonomically shaped tub interior, combined with optional headrest and neck support, makes extended bathing sessions more comfortable — reducing patient resistance and improving the overall bathing experience.

This is particularly valuable in memory care, palliative care, and long-term care environments where bathing can be anxiety-provoking for residents. A more comfortable bathing experience supports person-centered care goals.

## Who Is This For?

- Long-term care and memory care where the bathing experience directly affects resident wellbeing
- Palliative and hospice care environments where comfort is a primary care goal
- Rehabilitation facilities where therapeutic bathing (whirlpool, hydrotherapy) is part of the treatment plan
- Assisted living facilities that market person-centered care as a differentiator

## Therapeutic Options

The Comfortline supports optional whirlpool and air spa systems, allowing it to serve therapeutic bathing functions for patients with musculoskeletal conditions, post-surgical recovery, or chronic pain management needs.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is the whirlpool option a clinical medical device or a comfort feature?**
The whirlpool and air spa options are comfort and therapeutic features — not clinical medical devices. They are appropriate where hydrotherapy is included in a resident's care plan, or where the facility's care model includes therapeutic bathing as a quality-of-life service. For clinical-grade hydrotherapy protocols, consult your occupational therapist or physiotherapist for appropriate specifications.

**Is the Comfortline appropriate for memory care patients who resist bathing?**
Yes. The Comfortline's design — warmer surfaces, ergonomic interior shaping, padded headrest, and the option for a gentle warm soak — reduces the physical discomfort of bathing that can trigger resistance in patients with dementia. Consistent, comfortable bathing routines support both resident dignity and caregiver safety.

**Can the headrest and neck support be removed for easier cleaning?**
Yes — the headrest and comfort inserts are removable. Consult the IFU for the cleaning protocol for comfort accessories.
