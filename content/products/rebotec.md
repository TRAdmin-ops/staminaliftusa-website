## Rebotec Product Categories

**Shower & Commode Chairs** — From basic shower chairs to fully reclining hydraulic and electric models, including the Bonn, Berlin, Miami, Frankfurt, Hamburg, Phoenix, and Augsburg lines.

**Pediatric Walkers & Walking Frames** — Indoor walking frames and forearm walkers including the Fox Yano, Fixi Yano, Walk On, and Pluto models.

**Pediatric Crutches** — The Fun Kids and Safe In Kids forearm crutches, ergonomically designed for pediatric patients.

## About Rebotec

[Rebotec GmbH](https://www.rebotec.de/en/) has been manufacturing mobility aids in Germany for over 40 years. Products are manufactured to ISO 13485 and ISO 14971 standards. TR Group, Inc. provides domestic US distribution, warranty service, and local support for all Rebotec products.
