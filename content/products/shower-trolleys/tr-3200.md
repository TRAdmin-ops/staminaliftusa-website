## Overview

The TR-3200 provides full electric control of both height and tilt via a single hand control pendant — giving caregivers precise one-handed positioning during patient bathing. The extended height range (20–35 in) accommodates a broader range of caregiver heights and enables a lower floor position for easier lateral patient transfers from beds.

The 507 lbs (230 kg) weight capacity and choice of standard or long mattress surface make the TR-3200 a versatile standard-capacity option for a wide range of patient populations.

## Who Is This For?

The TR-3200 is specified by facilities that need:

- Full electric control of both height and tilt (upgrade over TR-3000's manual tilt)
- Greater height range than the TR-3000 (useful for mixed caregiver height profiles)
- Higher weight capacity than the standard 440 lbs (200 kg) TR-3000

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why does the TR-3200 have a lower minimum height than the TR-3000?**
The TR-3200 reaches 20 in at its lowest vs. 24 in for the TR-3000. This lower floor position makes it easier to perform lateral transfers directly from a bed — the trolley surface can sit at or below bed height, reducing the lift required during patient transfer.

**Is the TR-3200 available in a longer size for larger patients?**
Yes. The TR-3200 Long version (85 × 31 in) provides both a longer and wider mattress surface for taller patients who need more support. For patients above 507 lbs (230 kg), the TR-4200 Atlas Junior and TR-4000 Atlas are the appropriate bariatric specifications.

**When should I choose the TR-3200 over the TR-3000?**
Choose the TR-3200 if: (1) you need electric tilt as well as electric height, (2) your facility's patient mix includes patients in the 440–507 lbs (200–230 kg) range, or (3) your caregiver team spans a wide height range and benefits from the extended 20–35 in working height range. The TR-3000 covers most standard-capacity requirements where manual tilt is acceptable.
