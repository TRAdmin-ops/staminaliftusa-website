## Overview

The Bure Rise & Go DB combines two functions normally provided by separate devices: a powered standing assist mechanism (which helps the user rise from a chair) and a walking frame / rollator for ambulation support. Users who need help standing but can then walk independently need only one device rather than two.

The adjustable width (26–42 in / 66–106 cm) accommodates different body sizes and enables the device to pass through standard doorways while providing appropriate support for larger users.

Engineered in Sweden by DHG, the Rise & Go DB reflects Scandinavian design values — functional, durable, and IP65 rated for use in wet environments.

## Who Is This For?

- Home care and assisted living users who have difficulty rising from seated but can walk with support
- Rehabilitation programs focused on restoring independent standing and walking
- Users who want to maximize independence with minimal caregiver intervention

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Who prescribes the Bure Rise & Go DB — does it require a clinical referral?**
In clinical settings, powered walking aids are typically selected and recommended by a physical therapist or occupational therapist as part of a rehabilitation or mobility assessment. For home care purchases, local regulations vary — your authorized dealer can advise on applicable requirements in your state or province.

**What is the minimum standing ability required to use the Rise & Go DB?**
The Rise & Go DB provides powered assist for rising from a seated position — it does not perform the full transfer. Users need sufficient lower body strength and trunk stability to complete the stand with powered assist. The appropriate candidate assessment should be made by a physical or occupational therapist.

**Can the Rise & Go DB be used outdoors?**
The IP65 rating means it is protected against water jets, making it suitable for wet bathroom environments. For outdoor use, surface conditions (pavement, carpet, thresholds) and wheel type should be considered — discuss with your authorized dealer.

**Is the adjustable width needed for door clearance?**
Yes — the adjustable width (26–42 in / 66–106 cm) allows the device to be narrowed for passing through standard doorways and widened to the appropriate support width for the user's body. This dual function is one of the design advantages of the adjustable frame.
