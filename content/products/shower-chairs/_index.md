Accessible bathing requires more than a waterproof seat. Clinically rated shower chairs must support patient weight safely, provide adjustable positioning, and withstand daily use in demanding healthcare environments. TR Equipment and Rebotec products are engineered precisely for this — no consumer-grade construction, no design compromises.

The TR-1000 shower chair and TR-2810 thermostatic shower panel are designed to work together and in combination with TR Equipment shower trolleys. Rebotec's range extends coverage to commode chairs, reclining models, and pediatric seating.

All products are available in North America through authorized TR Group, Inc. dealer partners.
