Safe patient transfer — from bed to trolley, trolley to bath, chair to standing — is one of the highest injury-risk tasks in clinical care. The TR9650 mobile bath lift system eliminates the manual lifting and awkward positioning that cause musculoskeletal injuries, replacing them with powered, controlled transfer that protects both patient and caregiver.

The TR9650 requires no ceiling track installation, making it immediately deployable in existing facilities without structural modification. All three configurations — chair lift, stretcher lift, and combi lift — are foot-operated and convert between modes without tools.

Designed and manufactured in Sweden. Available in North America through authorized TR Group, Inc. dealer partners.

**Common questions:**
- **Do I need a ceiling track system?** — No. The TR9650 is a mobile unit that operates without any fixed installation.
- **What is the difference between the chair, stretcher, and combi configurations?** — Chair lift supports seated transfer. Stretcher lift supports supine transfer. Combi combines both functions in a single unit.
- **What is the weight capacity?** — 330 lbs (150 kg) across all configurations.
