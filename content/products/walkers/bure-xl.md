## Overview

The Bure XL is DHG's bariatric-capacity powered walking aid, providing the same stand-and-walk functionality as the Rise & Go DB at a 529 lbs (240 kg) working load and a 35 in (88 cm) wide support platform. For users who exceed the 330 lbs (150 kg) capacity of the standard Rise & Go DB, the Bure XL is the appropriate clinical specification.

The wider frame accommodates bariatric body widths while maintaining the device's core function: powered sit-to-stand assistance combined with a stable walking frame for subsequent ambulation.

## When to Specify the Bure XL

- User weight between 330–529 lbs (150–240 kg)
- Wider body width requiring the 35 in (88 cm) frame
- Bariatric rehabilitation programs where powered standing assist is clinically indicated

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is the Bure XL appropriate for bariatric rehabilitation programs?**
Yes. The Bure XL is specifically designed for bariatric users requiring powered standing assist — the 529 lbs (240 kg) capacity and 35 in (88 cm) wide frame provide appropriate support at body widths that exceed the standard Rise & Go DB's adjustable range. Suitability for a specific patient should be confirmed with the treating physiotherapist.

**Does the Bure XL fit through standard bariatric-configured doorways?**
The Bure XL has a 35 in (88 cm) wide fixed frame. Standard bariatric room door widths in US healthcare settings (typically 44–48 in / 112–122 cm clear) accommodate the XL frame. Confirm doorway widths in the specific facility before specifying.

**What is the clinical difference between the Bure XL and the Rise & Go DB beyond capacity?**
The primary difference is the fixed 35 in (88 cm) frame of the XL versus the adjustable 26–42 in (66–106 cm) frame of the Rise & Go DB. For bariatric users, the fixed wider frame provides consistent lateral support. For users who need the device to narrow for door clearance and widen for use, the Rise & Go DB's adjustability is the advantage — but only up to 330 lbs (150 kg) capacity.
