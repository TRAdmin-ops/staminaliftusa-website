## Overview

The TR-1700 is TR Equipment's space saving bathtub — designed to deliver height-adjustable electric bathing in a significantly more compact footprint than the TR-900 series (66 in vs. 92 in length). This makes it well-suited for smaller bathrooms and en-suite configurations common in newer care facility builds.

Electric height adjustment via push-button pendant reduces caregiver effort during bathing sessions and enables precise working height without manual pumping.

## Specifying the TR-1700 vs. TR-900

| | TR-1700 | TR-900 |
|---|---|---|
| Length | 66 in | 92 in |
| Width | 27 in | 33 in |
| Height adjustment | Electric | Hand control |
| Capacity | 330 lbs (150 kg) | 330 lbs (150 kg) |

Choose the TR-1700 when:
- Room size is limited and a compact footprint is required
- Electric height adjustment is preferred over manual control
- Caregiver ergonomics policy requires powered height adjustment

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much smaller is the TR-1700 compared to the TR-900?**
The TR-1700 is about 25 in shorter (66 in vs. 92 in) and about 5 in narrower (27 in vs. 33 in) than the TR-900. In a bathroom with limited floor area, this difference is often the deciding factor.

**Is electric height adjustment significantly easier than hand control?**
In high-volume bathing environments, the difference is meaningful. Electric adjustment eliminates the physical pumping action required by the TR-900's hand control, reduces caregiver fatigue over many bathing sessions, and adjusts faster — important where throughput and time per patient matter.

**Does the TR-1700 work with hoist and transfer accessories?**
Yes. The TR-1700 is compatible with TR Equipment hoisting and transfer accessories for patients who cannot independently step in or out of the tub. Contact us to discuss a complete bathing room specification.
