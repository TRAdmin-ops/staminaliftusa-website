For patients who benefit from immersion bathing — therapeutic warmth, relaxation, wound care, or simply the comfort of a full bath — TR Equipment bathing systems deliver clinical-grade functionality without sacrificing patient dignity.

Every model provides height adjustment so caregivers can work at ergonomically correct posture throughout the bathing and transfer process. This is Swedish design at its most practical: the equipment adapts to the caregiver, not the other way around.

Seven models cover the spectrum from compact residential installations to full therapeutic systems for acute and long-term care. All are manufactured in Sweden and available in North America through authorized TR Group, Inc. dealer partners.

**Common questions:**
- **What is the difference between the TR-900 and TR-1700?** — The TR-900 is a height-adjustable bathtub suited to general clinical use. The TR-1700 is designed for patients requiring more extensive caregiver assistance, with greater access and positioning capability.
- **Which system is right for a nursing home vs. a hospital?** — The Ecoline and Comfortline suit long-term care environments well. The Spaceline and TR-1700 are designed for higher-acuity settings. Contact an authorized dealer to match the right model to your facility layout and patient population.
- **Do the systems require permanent installation?** — Yes — TR Equipment bathing systems are fixed installations. Discuss infrastructure requirements with your dealer before specifying.
