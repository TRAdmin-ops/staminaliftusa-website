## Overview

The TR-900 Stainless Steel shares the same height-adjustable bathing platform as the standard TR-900, but replaces the glass fibre tub with a full stainless steel construction. This makes it the preferred choice for:

- Isolation units or infection control environments where glass fibre is contraindicated
- Burn units requiring frequent high-concentration disinfection and full surface sterilization
- Facilities using high-concentration disinfectants incompatible with glass fibre
- Memory care or behavioral health units where impact resistance is a concern
- Facilities with previous experience of tub damage or staining

## Infection Control Considerations

Stainless steel is the material of choice in surgical and intensive care environments precisely because it tolerates a broader range of disinfectants, is non-porous, and is easier to visually inspect for cleanliness. For bathing systems in high-risk infection control settings, specifying stainless is the conservative choice.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What disinfectants are compatible with the stainless steel tub?**
The stainless steel tub is compatible with a broader range of hospital-grade disinfectants than glass fibre, including chlorine-based products. The specific approved cleaning agents and dilutions are listed in the product IFU — always follow the IFU for disinfection protocols.

**Is the TR-900 Stainless Steel appropriate for isolation unit or burn unit installation?**
Yes. The stainless steel construction is specifically chosen for infection-sensitive environments — isolation units, burn units, oncology wards, and any setting where MRSA, C. diff, or other notifiable organism decontamination protocols are in effect. Burn units in particular benefit from the full sterilization compatibility and non-porous surface, which withstands the aggressive cleaning regimens required in those environments.

**Are the dimensions identical to the standard TR-900?**
Yes. The TR-900 Stainless Steel and standard TR-900 share the same 92 × 33 in footprint and 24–37 in height range. The only material difference is the tub construction.
